\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We present \textbf{\ourmethod}, a monocular 4D human--object reconstruction framework centered on observation-anchored iterative view expansion.
The method expands camera coverage through short, reliable generation hops while preserving the input video as the highest-confidence supervision signal.
It further aligns fixed-size generation windows with variable-length HOI states using two-level semantic chunking, targeting the disoccluded regions where text-conditioned generation is most consequential.
The remaining work before submission is to complete the implementation-dependent confidence weighting and to fill the targeted ablation tables that verify non-collapse, disocclusion-region gains, and state-of-the-art reconstruction quality.
